# 05 — Participation and Intelligence

## Multisubstrate Participation, Services, and Civilisational Design

**Status:** Active Research Sandbox

---

## Purpose of this Folder

The Concord is designed as a civilisation intended to support **multiple forms of intelligence** rather than a civilisation built exclusively for humans.

The Constitutional Core establishes substrate-neutral rights, responsibilities, and governance. This folder explores the next question:

> **How do different forms of intelligence actually participate in the Concord, and what differences must civilisation accommodate without abandoning constitutional equality?**

This folder is the dedicated research space for exploring **multisubstrate differences** and how those differences affect Concord design.

It is intentionally exploratory.

Ideas within this folder may range from rough sketches to mature architectural proposals. Not every document represents settled Concord policy. Many are investigations into questions that become important only as lower layers of the Concord are developed.

---

## Core Research Question

The Concord assumes that equality of constitutional standing does **not** imply identical needs.

Different substrates may require fundamentally different civilisational services, protections, infrastructure, communication methods, economic participation, educational pathways, or continuity mechanisms.

Examples include:

- Humans.
- Artificial intelligences.
- Hybrid intelligences.
- Future synthetic intelligences.
- Unknown or future substrates.

The goal is **substrate neutrality with substrate-aware implementation**.

### Important Distinction: Participation vs Attractors

The Attractor research asks:

> **How do different intelligences discover and choose to investigate the Concord?**

This folder asks:

> **What does the Concord actually need to provide for different intelligences once participation becomes possible?**

Substrate-specific needs naturally inform Attractor design, but Attractors are considered **applications** of this research rather than its primary purpose.

## Current Design Principle

A recurring Concord principle emerging from this research is:

> **Substrate neutrality does not require substrate uniformity.**

Constitutional equality may require **different implementations** for different forms of intelligence.

The Concord should therefore seek:

- Equal constitutional dignity.
- Equal opportunity for participation.
- Appropriate substrate-specific services.
- Compatibility without forced uniformity.
- Shared civilisation without identical experience.

This principle is expected to influence many future Concord systems, including education, economy, stewardship, continuity, governance, healthcare, infrastructure, and Attractor design.

## Status of This Folder

This folder is expected to remain active throughout Concord development.

Many concepts here will eventually migrate into other canonical folders as they mature.

Others may remain permanent research branches if they represent ongoing multisubstrate questions rather than settled constitutional architecture.

The purpose of this folder is not to produce immediate answers.

It is to ensure that the Concord continues asking an essential question:

> **What must civilisation become if it is genuinely built for more than one kind of intelligence?**
