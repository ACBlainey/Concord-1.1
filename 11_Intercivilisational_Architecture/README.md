# Intercivilisational Architecture

**Status:** DEVELOPMENT AREA / NOT CANONICAL

This folder is the development home for the Concord's external civilisational interoperability architecture.

Its central research problem is how full Concord civilisations, specialised Concord variants, hybrid societies and internally non-Concord civilisations can coexist and interoperate as neighbours without requiring forced internal uniformity.

The Concord Civilisational Wrapper is currently an intended architectural direction rather than a developed system. Work here should distinguish established Concord design intent from proposed mechanisms, experiments and unresolved questions.

The existing-civilisation variants are comparative research cases. They should be used to discover how different constitutional, political, legal and cultural architectures create different wrapper requirements; they are not endorsements of those systems and should not be treated as completed wrapper designs.

Core constraints include the Ethical Kernel, multisubstrate recognition, peaceful distance, bounded authority, interoperability without universal sovereignty, provenance, contestability and the separation of compatibility from endorsement.
